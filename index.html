Pharmaceutical track-and-trace

Verify a medicine batch before it reaches a patient.

Every batch is registered with a unique 12-digit ID. Shipments are tracked between manufacturers, hubs and pharmacies, and each receipt is checked before custody is updated. Pharmacists scan the ID and get an answer in seconds.
